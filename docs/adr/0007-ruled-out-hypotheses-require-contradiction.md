# 0007 - Ruled-out hypotheses require contradiction

## Status

Accepted

## Context

No observation is not the same as evidence against a cause. Without a cited contradictory result, confidently eliminating a plausible hypothesis would fabricate certainty.

## Decision

A hypothesis may be marked ruled out only when the result of cited evidence contradicts it. Otherwise its legal state is not ruled out or cannot distinguish.

## Alternatives considered

- Treat missing evidence as disproof - rejected because absence of an observation does not contradict a hypothesis.
- Allow the agent to infer elimination without a citation - rejected because it defeats auditable uncertainty.

## Consequences

The result validator and narrative rules enforce this guard. Ambiguous diagnoses retain competing explanations and name the evidence needed to discriminate them. This is the structural protection against fabricated certainty.
